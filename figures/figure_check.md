# Figure check

Measured from the figure files by scripts/make_figures.py. Open Research Europe asks for RGB colour, a white background, text of at least 8 pt in a consistent typeface, a printed width of 75 to 150 mm, and EPS files or uncompressed TIFF files of at least 600 dpi. The PNG files (300 dpi) are those placed in the Word and LaTeX manuscripts.
